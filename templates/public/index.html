Campus events are scattered across websites and inboxes. Eventer pulls them into one place so students can see what’s on today, where it is, and get a reminder before it starts.

What’s in the app

Campus map

Discover events happening across Dartmouth, right where they take place.

Filters that fit your day

Browse by day, category, or search so the events you care about find you.

Reminders

Set a local reminder on any event and get a ping before it starts. No account required.

Works offline

Events stay cached on your phone, so you can still browse when campus Wi‑Fi drops.
