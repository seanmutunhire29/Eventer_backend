A Tuesday night on campus

  • Free pizza, Collis Common Ground 6:30 PM
  • Open mixer, Sigma Delt 7:00 PM
  • A cappella show, Rollins Chapel 8:00 PM
  • Intramural hockey, Thompson Arena 9:00 PM

A typical night, pulled from four different clubs' pages. Eventer keeps a live version of this on your phone.

Campus events are scattered across websites, list-servs, and flyers stapled to kiosks. Eventer pulls them into one place, so you can see what's on today, where it is, and get a reminder before it starts.

What's inside

  • MAP

    Campus map

    See every event pinned right where it's happening.

  • FLTR

    Filters that fit your day

    Browse by day, category, or search, so the events you care about find you.

  • RMND

    Reminders

    Set a reminder on any event and get a ping before it starts. No account required.

  • OFLN

    Works offline

    Events stay cached on your phone, so a dead Wi-Fi spot won't stop you.
